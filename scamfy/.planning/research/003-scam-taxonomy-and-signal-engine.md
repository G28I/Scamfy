# Domain Research 003: Student Scam Taxonomy & Fraud Signal Matrix

- **Topic**: Scam Categories, Risk Signals & Indicators Affecting Students
- **Date**: 2026-09-23 (Updated 2026-09-24)

## Scam Taxonomy (Primary Categories)

1. **Part-Time Task / Job Fraud**:
   - YouTube like/subscribe tasks, Telegram review assignments, upfront registration/kit fees, fake recruitment offer letters, data-entry security deposits.
2. **Honey-Trap / Relationship-Based Financial Recruitment**:
   - Building rapid romantic/personal rapport or intimacy across dating apps, Instagram, or WhatsApp, followed by requests to invest in exclusive platforms, accept/forward payments, pay emergency fees, or share private credentials.
3. **Money-Mule / Account Renting**:
   - Asking student to receive funds via UPI/bank and forward to crypto/cash/another UPI for a 5–10% commission; renting out student bank accounts or UPI handles.
4. **Predatory Instant Digital Loan**:
   - Unregulated 7-day loan apps deducting 40% processing fees upfront, demanding contacts/gallery permissions, and using coercive/blackmail recovery tactics.
5. **Guaranteed-Return / Fake Investment Schemes**:
   - Promises of guaranteed fixed daily/monthly returns (e.g. "₹1,000 → 3.5% daily", "Double your money in 7 days"), fake trading dashboards, VIP Telegram stock/forex tip channels, Ponzi pyramid structures, and crypto trading pools.
6. **Urgent Impersonation & Digital Arrest**:
   - Fake police/customs/courier/TRAI calls threatening arrest or legal action, fake parcel contraband claims, demanding immediate transfer to "verification/clearing accounts".
7. **Marketplace & Advance Fee Fraud**:
   - OLX/marketplace QR code payment scams ("Scan to receive money"), fake advance delivery charges, fake buyer escrow demands.

## High-Confidence Deterministic Signals
- "Pay security deposit/registration fee before joining or receiving assignment"
- "Scan QR code or enter UPI PIN to receive money / verify credit"
- "Send back 90% and keep 10% for handling / commission"
- "Guaranteed 100% risk-free fixed daily return (e.g. 3.5% daily, 50% monthly)"
- "My uncle/friend has an insider investment platform with guaranteed profits"
- Download of third-party `.apk` files from untrusted links or external drives
- Requests for OTP / Netbanking password / UPI PIN / Aadhaar OTP under pressure
